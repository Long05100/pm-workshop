Input 4 · Risk

What can go wrong?

Step 1

Identify

Which risks do we see?

Step 2

Assess

How likely and how bad?

Likely, and without mould no production.

Step 3

Respond

What do we do about it?

Freeze the design early, agree a fixed date with the toolmaker.
